# 🛒 Retail Customer Trends & Behavioral Analytics

A comprehensive data analytics pipeline analyzing 3,900 customer transactions to uncover purchasing patterns, revenue drivers, product discount dependency, and customer segmentation. Built using **Python (Pandas/Seaborn)** for exploratory data analysis (EDA) and cleaning, **SQL** for relational business queries, and **Power BI** for executive dashboard storytelling.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary & Key Findings

* **Revenue Distribution:** Male demographic segment accounts for $157,890 in revenue compared to $75,191 from female shoppers across 3,900 transaction records.
* **Subscription Dynamics:** Non-subscribers make up ~73% of the total customer base (2,847 buyers). While average order value (~$59) remains balanced across both cohorts, overall volume drives revenue from non-subscribers.
* **Discount Dependency:** High promotion sensitivity identified in specific categories—Hats (50%), Sneakers (49.66%), and Coats (49.07%) exhibit high discount reliance for purchase completion.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Core customer base shows strong loyalty (3,116 loyal buyers vs. 701 returning and 83 new buyers), presenting an opportunity for retention-focused loyalty migration strategies.
